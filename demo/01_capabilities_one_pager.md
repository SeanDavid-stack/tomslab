## What's in the app

### Search — three ways
- **Keyword** — full-text over every message; `@author` syntax to scope by poster.
- **Semantic** — meaning-based; finds concepts even when the words don't match.
- **Visual** — image-text search on chart screenshots ("chart with three clear peaks").

### Ask Tom
Multi-turn Q&A grounded only in the imported material. Every answer shows citations (message IDs, PDF page numbers) that jump straight to the source. If it isn't in the material, it says it doesn't know.

### Organise the archive
- **Favourites** — right-click an author's avatar to promote their posts (gold ★).
- **Bookmarks** — star any message and add a private note.
- **Concepts** — one-click filter by concept (VPOC, IBL, absorption, etc.), seeded from the Glossary PDF.

### Browse visually
- **Gallery** — grid of every chart image in the export; click through to the post it came from.
- **Featured speaker highlighting** — the primary author's posts are visually separated from replies.

### Sources the app can ingest
- DiscordChatExporter JSON exports
- PDFs (glossaries, structured trade guides, market-structure references)
- YouTube videos (transcripts only; click-through opens YouTube)

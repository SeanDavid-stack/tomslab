# Demo script — Tom meeting, April 2026

**Total runtime:** 45 min (can stretch to 60). Live app, PM-led.
**Goal:** show progress, get Tom's call on 7 blocking decisions, keep him engaged.
**Tone:** conversational, PM-to-SME. You're showing, not selling. Let Tom react. Don't defend.

**Legend:**
- 🗣 *italic in quotes* = suggested script, say it roughly like this
- 🖱 = do this in the app
- ❓ = question beat — ask, then **shut up** and let Tom answer. Write it down.

---

## 00:00 — Frame (2 min)

🗣 *"Tom, thanks for the time. Before I show you anything: this is a free desktop app — no fee, no support, users run it themselves on their own machine. Nothing phones home. The only material in it is stuff a user has already downloaded themselves from the Discord pins and the PDFs you've posted. It's built by SDE-Software as a side project, totally separate from any paid work."*

🗣 *"I'll walk you through what it does in about thirty minutes, then I have a short list of decisions I need your call on. Sound good?"*

🖱 Open the app cold. The splash screen appears, then the disclaimer dialog opens on top of the main window. **Pause here** on the splash / disclaimer — don't click through yet. Let Tom see exactly what a first-run user sees.

❓ *"Before I go any deeper — quick one. Are you OK with the app being called 'Tom's Lab' and this splash the way it is? If the name changes, the splash, the title bar, the docs, all of it changes. Easier to know now."*

*(Questions 2 and 3 in Doc B. If he wants a rename, write the preferred name down verbatim and continue the demo — don't redo anything on the fly. If he says fine, move on.)*

🖱 Click through the disclaimer (scroll to the bottom to enable the accept button).

---

## 00:03 — Cold-open: Ask Tom (5 min)
**This is the highest-impact beat. Lead with it. Everything else is supporting cast.**

🗣 *"First thing I want your reaction to. A user can ask a plain-English question and the app answers using your material — only your material — with citations back to the exact source."*

🖱 Click **Ask Tom** tab. Type:
> Explain Naked VPOC (NVPOC) and why Tom watches for it.

*(NVPOC is a signature Tom term — it pulls heavily from his Discord posts, the Glossary PDF, and his YouTube videos, so the answer will show all three citation types in one go. Mean Reversion Structured Trade was tested and is too PDF-dominant — Tom uses "MR" / "mean rev" shorthand in Discord, not the full phrase, so the keyword search misses Discord entirely.)*

🖱 Wait for the answer. When citations appear:
🖱 Click a `[msg:…]` link — it jumps to the Discord message in Feed. Point it out.
🖱 Click a `[doc:…]` link — it jumps to the Glossary PDF page. Point it out.
🖱 If a `[vid:…]` link is present, click it — it opens the YouTube video at the right timestamp. Point it out.

🗣 *"Everything it says is grounded in something you wrote. If it's not in the material, it says so instead of guessing."*

❓ *"If a user ran this and got something wrong, how would you want us to handle it?"* (This is **Question 9**. Get his answer now.)

---

## 00:08 — Search, three ways (6 min)

🗣 *"Now the basics — search. Three modes."*

🖱 Switch search mode to **Keyword**. Type `VPOC`. Hit enter.
🗣 *"Classic search. Instant. Highlights your matches."*

🖱 Switch to **Semantic**. Type:
> how to handle overnight inventory
🗣 *"Meaning-based. Finds posts about the concept even when the user doesn't know your exact words."*

🖱 Switch to **Visual**. Type:
> chart with three clear peaks
🗣 *"Image search. For when a user remembers what a chart looked like but not which post it came from."*

❓ *"Are any of these three the wrong thing to put in front of a new user?"*

---

## 00:14 — Your voice, filtered (4 min)

🗣 *"The archive has everyone's posts, but your voice is the point."*

🖱 Scroll the Feed. Point out gold-highlighted messages.
🗣 *"Your posts are visually separated from replies."*

🖱 Click a concept chip above the search bar — e.g. **VPOC** or **IBL**.
🗣 *"One click filters to that concept. The chips are seeded from your Glossary PDF, so it's your vocabulary, not ours."*

🖱 Right-click another trader's avatar → **Save favourite**. Show gold ★ appearing on their posts.
🗣 *"A user can promote other high-signal voices from the channel so their posts are easy to find later."*

❓ *"Any authors you don't want surfaced at all, even as replies?"* (This is **Question 7**.)

---

## 00:18 — Gallery (3 min)

🖱 Click **Gallery** tab. Show the grid of charts.

🗣 *"Every chart image from the export, browsable as a grid. Click one…"*

🖱 Click a chart → jumps back to the Feed post it came from.

🗣 *"…and you're at the post. Good for 'I remember a chart but not the conversation.'"*

*(Short beat — don't linger.)*

## 00:21 — Bookmarks (2 min)

🖱 Click the ★ icon on a message → add a note → open **Bookmarks** tab.

🗣 *"User's personal scratchpad. Save anything, add notes, come back later."*

## 00:23 — Ingest — the trust beat (4 min)
**This one matters for Tom's relationship with Bookmap. Don't rush.**

🗣 *"This part is important. Nothing is in the app until the user puts it there. They run DiscordChatExporter themselves — we don't fetch anything, we don't have any credentials, we never touch Discord. They drop the JSON file in, PDFs go in a folder, that's the data."*

🖱 Show **File → Import**. Point out the `_assets/docs` folder.

🗣 *"This matters for your position with Bookmap. The user owns their export. The user owns whatever ToS exposure exists. We just help them search their own download."*

❓ *"Have you talked to Bookmap about this?"* (This is **Question 6**.)

## 00:28 — The decisions (14 min)
**This is the actual purpose of the meeting. Walk through Doc B out loud.**

🗣 *"OK — the reason I wanted us to talk. I've got nine things I need your call on before this goes anywhere. None of them are long."*

Open Doc B on screen (or keep the printed copy in front of you). Read each question, give him the options, **stop talking, write his answer**. Don't defend, don't soften, don't argue a direction. You're collecting data.

Questions in order:
1. Handoff mechanism
2. App name *(already asked at 00:00 — confirm his answer)*
3. Splash screen *(already asked at 00:00 — confirm)*
4. Attribution copy
5. "Ask Tom" feature name
6. Bookmap ToS *(already asked at 00:23 — confirm)*
7. Content exclusions *(already asked at 00:14 — confirm)*
8. YouTube / TomTube *(already asked at 00:27 — confirm)*
9. Wrong-answer handling *(already asked at 00:03 — confirm)*

*Because you've asked six of these inline during the demo, this segment will be closer to 4–6 minutes of fresh questions plus confirmations. That's fine — you're under time.*

If there's slack, walk the should-answer questions (10–14). Skip 15–17 unless he brings them up.
